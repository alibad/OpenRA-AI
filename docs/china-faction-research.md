# China faction research

**Research cutoff:** 2026-08-12  
**Implementation scope:** fictional, balanced World War III RTS systems. This
faction does not reproduce a real attack, target a real location, use a real
leader, or portray a real person as its hero.

## Dated capability evidence

- **2024-04-19 — network and information support.** China's Ministry of
  National Defense described the newly established Information Support Force as
  a strategic arm underpinning coordinated development and use of networked
  information systems. The same announcement placed it alongside the
  Aerospace, Cyberspace, and Joint Logistics Support forces in the revised
  service structure. Source: [Ministry of National Defense, "Remarks ...
  Concerning the PLA Information Support Force"](https://eng.mod.gov.cn/xb/News_213114/NewsRelease/16302635.html).
- **2024-11-11 — crewed aircraft as command-and-control nodes.** A Ministry of
  National Defense article quoting the state-owned aircraft manufacturer
  described the twin-seat J-20S role set as air superiority, precision strike,
  situational awareness, electronic jamming, tactical command and control, and
  teaming with unmanned aircraft. Source: [Ministry of National Defense,
  "Twin-seat J-20S stealth fighter to be unveiled"](https://eng.mod.gov.cn/xb/News_213114/TopStories/16351084.html).
- **2025-01-22 — networked multi-role surface combatants.** The Ministry of
  National Defense reported that the first Type 054B frigate entered service
  with advances in stealth, combat-command systems, firepower integration, and
  diverse task-force missions. Source: [Ministry of National Defense, "China's
  first Type 054B frigate Luohe commissioned"](https://eng.mod.gov.cn/xb/MilitaryServices/News_213106/16365694.html).
- **2025-03-01 — combined-arms command.** China Military Online reported a live
  exercise by an 81st Group Army combined-arms brigade intended to improve
  grass-roots command of combined operations. Source: [China Military Online,
  "PLA Army's Combined-arms Brigade Conducts Live-fire Exercise"](https://eng.chinamil.com.cn/BILINGUAL/News_209203/16372137.html).
- **2025-05-19 — joint land/air/naval and unmanned training.** The Golden Dragon
  2025 exercise combined naval and air activity with land and air activity; its
  published subjects included assault-vehicle coordination, fast roping, and
  unmanned equipment operations, supported by an amphibious dock landing ship,
  frigates, helicopters, radar troops, and a joint logistics/training center.
  Source: [China Military Online, "China-Cambodia 'Golden Dragon 2025' joint
  exercise kicks off"](https://eng.chinamil.com.cn/CHINA_209163/Exercises/News_209184/16387206.html).
- **2025-12-23 — current authoritative capability overview.** The U.S.
  Department of Defense's 2025 annual report describes continued combined-arms
  modernization with modern armor, artillery, and rocket systems; air and
  ground unmanned systems in amphibious/urban training; missile forces for
  conventional precision strike; a large surface and amphibious shipbuilding
  base; and the Type 076 amphibious assault ship as a likely UAV carrier. This
  is an external government assessment, not a Chinese primary claim. Source:
  [U.S. Department of Defense, 2025 China Military Power Report (PDF)](https://media.defense.gov/2025/Dec/23/2003849070/-1/-1/1/ANNUALREPORT-TO-CONGRESS-MILITARY-AND-SECURITY-DEVELOPMENTS-INVOLVING-THEPEOPLES-REPUBLIC-OF-CHINA-2025.PDF).
- **2026-02-03 and 2026-04-21 — manned-unmanned teaming at unit level.** Two
  China Military Online releases describe UGVs and reconnaissance drones used
  for reconnaissance/fire-suppression guidance, followed by training that
  integrated unmanned equipment into air reconnaissance and strike,
  barrier-breaching, and three-dimensional assault. Sources: ["PLA Army Brigade
  Conducts Manned-unmanned Training"](https://eng.chinamil.com.cn/2025xb/M_251449/V_251467/16440805.html) and
  ["Manned-Unmanned Coordination Training"](https://eng.chinamil.com.cn/2025xb/C_251453/TE/16456345.html).
- **2026-05-08 — rotary-wing response, assault, and precision strike.** China
  Military Online described Z-20 and Z-10 training for quick response, air
  assault, and precision strike. Source: [China Military Online, "Army
  Helicopters Engage in Combat Training"](https://eng.chinamil.com.cn/2025xb/M_251449/V_251467/16459363.html).
- **2026-07-23 — amphibious aviation and well-deck operations.** China Military
  Online reported Type 075 training covering shipborne helicopter operations,
  LCAC well-dock operations, emergency response, and damage control. Source:
  [China Military Online, "PLAN Amphibious Assault Ship in All-Element Combat
  Training"](https://eng.chinamil.com.cn/2025xb/C_251453/TE/16475458.html).

## RTS translation

The evidence supports roles, not an attempt to simulate classified performance
or reproduce a specific contingency. The game therefore uses fictionalized
names and intentionally compressed ranges, payloads, costs, and survivability.

| Evidence-backed role | In-game system | Balance boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Networked information support | Battlefield Network Specialist deploys a local sensor/jammer field; Red Spear supplies a short command-network aura | Short radius, immobile while deployed, modest bonuses; no global reveal or permanent disable |
| Combined-arms brigade | Riflemen, missile teams, Qilin tanks, ZBD-05-inspired amphibious IFVs, Longbow rocket launchers, and aircraft cover complementary target classes | No single generalist deletes every armor class; support units are fragile and expensive |
| Manned-unmanned teaming | Lynx UGV, Cloud Shadow UAV, and long-range drone payloads on the Haiwang assault ship | Drones trade durability and ammunition for scouting or precision; they must rearm or are build-limited by cost |
| Precision fires and missile forces | Longbow modular rocket launcher and portable AA/AT team role toggle | Long reloads, minimum ranges, visible projectiles, and vulnerable launch platforms preserve counterplay |
| Air assault and tactical command | J-20S-inspired Sky Spear fighter and Z-20T-inspired Crane helicopter | Limited ammunition, pad rearming, and role-specific weapons prevent persistent map control |
| Amphibious integration | Amphibious IFV plus a conventional landing craft and mission-scripted beachhead | Water speed is lower than road speed; the IFV is weaker than the main battle tank |
| Networked frigate and UAV-capable assault ship | Luyang network frigate and fictional Haiwang drone assault ship | Frigate is an escort rather than a battleship; the assault ship is expensive, slow, and needs escorts |

## Fiction and safety decisions

- **Red Spear is fictional.** The callsign, face, voice, biography, and abilities
  do not refer to a real leader or service member. The unit is a command-network
  specialist with a precision rifle, not a Tanya clone: it has no C4 demolition,
  instant building deletion, or dual-pistol behavior.
- The scenario is set on the fictional **Haitan Test Range** against the
  fictional **Obsidian Directorate**. Objectives concern relay nodes, a beach
  landing, and a combined-arms counterattack rather than a historical or current
  real-world strike plan.
- Weapon ranges and damage are balanced relative to native Red Alert units and
  do not claim real-world range, penetration, stealth, electronic-warfare, or
  sensor performance.
