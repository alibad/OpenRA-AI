# Turkey faction research and editorial boundary

Research cutoff: **2026-08-12**. Sources were last accessed on that date.
This document records broad, publicly described military roles used to shape a
fictional RTS faction. It is not an order of battle, readiness assessment, or
claim about classified capability. Platform quantities, bases, tactics,
performance values, weapon ranges, damage, and production times are deliberately
not reproduced in game.

## Authoritative role evidence

### Mobile combined arms

- BMC describes ALTAY as Türkiye's main battle tank and lists a 120 mm gun,
  tracked cross-country mobility, protection, and command-and-control features.
  BMC's current program history says serial-production vehicles were delivered
  in October 2025. This supports a durable maneuver-tank role, not the in-game
  statistics. Sources: [BMC ALTAY overview](https://www.bmc.com.tr/en/defense-industry/altay),
  [BMC ALTAY technical specifications](https://www.bmc.com.tr/en/defense-industry/altay/technical-specifications).
- The same BMC product family describes FIRTINA as a mobile howitzer with a new
  fire-control system and automatic loading, and ALTUĞ as a modular 8x8 platform
  for protected transport, direct fire, and command-and-control roles. Otokar's
  ARMA 8x8 page likewise lists APC, IFV, mortar, command, reconnaissance,
  recovery, and other mission configurations. Sources:
  [BMC land-systems overview](https://www.bmc.com.tr/en/defense-industry/altay),
  [Otokar ARMA 8x8](https://defense.otokar.com.tr/wheeled-armored/8x8-armored/arma-8x8-armored).

The game translates these broad roles into the wholly fictional **Bozkır MBT**,
**Aras 8x8 IFV**, and **Yıldırım self-propelled howitzer**. The Mechanized
Rifleman's proximity bonus expresses infantry/transport cooperation without
claiming a real doctrine multiplier.

### Drones, reconnaissance, and designation

- Turkish Aerospace describes ANKA as a medium-altitude, long-endurance system
  for reconnaissance, target detection/identification, intelligence, electronic
  warfare, communications relay, and precision-guided weapons. The manufacturer
  reports more than 400,000 fleet flight hours on its current product page.
  [Turkish Aerospace ANKA](https://www.tusas.com/en/products/uav/operative-strategic-uav-systems/anka).
- Turkish Aerospace says AKSUNGUR is designed for continuous day/night ISR and
  attack with EO/IR, radar, SIGINT, and communications payloads. A dated company
  release records a 13 March 2025 laser-guided-munition test.
  [Turkish Aerospace AKSUNGUR](https://www.tusas.com/en/products/uav/high-payload-capacity-uas/aksungur),
  [13 March 2025 precision-strike release](https://www.tusas.com/en/media-center/news/aksungur-demonstrates-precision-strikes-with-lgk-81-and-lgk-82-munitions).
- Baykar's current AKINCI page explicitly describes land and maritime ISTAR,
  strike, coastal-patrol, surface-warfare, and anti-submarine roles. A 2022
  company report also records one UAV laser-marking a target for another,
  which is the bounded inspiration for the faction's designation mechanic.
  [Baykar AKINCI](https://baykartech.com/en/uav/bayraktar-akinci/),
  [4 July 2022 designation test](https://baykartech.com/en/press/turkish-combat-drone-aknc-undergoes-laser-guided-firing-test/).

The game uses the fictional **Kuzgun-M drone** and **Forward Drone Operator**.
Designation is a visible, short-duration damage-vulnerability condition with a
fragile human operator, giving opponents clear counterplay. It is not a model of
real sensor range, latency, or targeting procedure.

### Electronic warfare and layered air defence

- ASELSAN's June 2025 KORAL 200 brochure describes mobile electronic support and
  electronic attack, wide-frequency sensing, direction finding, jamming, and
  deception in support of suppression of enemy air defence. This supports a
  mobile EW-support role. [ASELSAN KORAL 200 brochure, June 2025](https://cdn.aselsan.com/aselsanweb-prod/documents/capabilities/products/brochure/koral-200/koral-200-en.pdf).
- ASELSAN's 2024 KORKUT release describes a stabilized 35 mm mobile system for
  mechanized-force defence against aircraft, helicopters, missiles, and UAVs.
  [ASELSAN KORKUT 8x8 release, 2024](https://wwwcdn.aselsan.com/api/file/ASELSAN_EUROSATORY_KORKUT_PressRelease.pdf).
- Roketsan describes HİSAR-A/HİSAR-O as a modular low/medium-altitude family for
  protecting troops, ports, bases, and facilities against fixed-wing,
  rotary-wing, missile, and UAV threats. Its IDEF 2025 release places HİSAR and
  SİPER inside a broader layered national air-defence portfolio.
  [Roketsan HİSAR](https://www.roketsan.com.tr/en/products/hisar-air-defence-missiles),
  [Roketsan IDEF 2025 portfolio](https://www.roketsan.com.tr/en/media/news/roketsan-made-powerful-impact-idef-2025-debut-cutting-edge-defense-systems).
- NATO's Integrated Air and Missile Defence Policy, endorsed 13 February 2025,
  emphasizes a coherent, resilient, 360-degree system integrating sensors,
  command-and-control, and effectors against threats from small UAS through
  complex missiles. [NATO IAMD Policy, 13 February 2025](https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/official_texts_233084.htm?selectedLocale=en).

The game abstracts these roles into the fictional **Sancak EW carrier** and
**Gök Kalkan mobile air-defence system**, backed by normal Red Alert static air
defences, a fighter, and air-defence-capable ships. Jamming is local, temporary,
and countered by range, direct fire, and scouting.

### Rotorcraft and fighter aviation

- Turkish Aerospace describes T129 ATAK roles including attack, armed
  reconnaissance, escort, precision strike, fire support, and electronic-warfare
  survivability. [Turkish Aerospace T129 ATAK](https://www.tusas.com/en/products/helicopter/co-development-production/t129-atak).
- The Turkish Ministry of National Defence 2025 activity report records ongoing
  F-16 structural and ÖZGÜR modernization work, while Turkish Aerospace describes
  the current F-16 fleet as the baseline that its future fighter program is meant
  to replace. These sources establish a continuing multirole-fighter requirement,
  not any in-game platform performance.
  [Turkish Ministry of National Defence 2025 activity report](https://www.msb.gov.tr/Content/Upload/Docs/2025%20YILI%20MSB%20FAAL%C4%B0YET%20RAPORU%281%29.pdf),
  [Turkish Aerospace fighter-development overview](https://www.tusas.com/en/products/aircraft/indigenous-development).

The in-game **Turna attack helicopter** and **Şahin-X multirole fighter** are
original fictional airframes. Their silhouettes, names, loadouts, and balance do
not reproduce T129, F-16, KAAN, or any other real aircraft.

### Amphibious mobility and naval control

- FNSS describes ZAHA/MAV as an armoured vehicle able to move from ship to shore,
  land troops under protection, and continue as a combat vehicle on land. Its
  2026 exercise account records Turkish marine units using MAVs in an integrated
  air-sea-land NATO exercise. Sources:
  [FNSS MAV role description](https://www.fnss.com.tr/en/media/press-releases/turkish-mod-awards-fnss-for-turkish-navys-armoured-amphibious-assault-vehicle-program),
  [FNSS Steadfast Dart 26 account](https://www.fnss.com.tr/en/newsletter/fnss-focus-q1-1976).
- NATO's 1 April 2025 Dynamic Mariner account lists Türkiye among six nations in
  a large exercise combining ships, submarines, aircraft, and amphibious units.
  NATO's Neptune Strike 25-1 announcement emphasizes freedom of navigation,
  securing maritime chokepoints, protecting underwater infrastructure, and
  sea-air-land integration. Sources:
  [NATO Dynamic Mariner 25](https://www.ncia.nato.int/newsroom/news/ncia-supports-dynamic-mariner-25-the-largest-naval-exercise-in-recent-years),
  [NATO Neptune Strike 25-1](https://sfn.nato.int/newsroom/news-archive/2025/nato-conducts-final-preparations-for-neptune-strike-2025.aspx).
- STM describes the İstif/I-class frigate as a networked air-, surface-, and
  anti-submarine-warfare design derived from Ada-class experience, and says the
  Ada class serves in air, surface, underwater, and electronic-warfare roles.
  [STM naval portfolio and service account, 2024](https://www.stm.com.tr/en/press-releases/stm-brings-high-tech-naval-solutions-euronaval).
- Meteksan records ULAQ armed unmanned surface-vehicle tests during the Turkish
  Navy's Sea Wolf 2021 exercise and describes configurable missile, EW, jamming,
  intelligence, mine-hunting, ASW, and rescue roles. This is manufacturer
  evidence for a general unmanned-surface role, not a claim that every payload
  is operational. [Meteksan ULAQ test account](https://www.meteksan.com/en/news/ulaq-successfully-hit-the-target).

The game uses the fictional **Deniz Kaplan amphibious carrier**,
**Marmara-class frigate**, **Ege-class corvette**, and **Poyraz USV**. They follow
native OpenRA naval movement, wake, targeting, yard-repair, turret, sinking, and
death contracts. No real ship, crew, or engagement is recreated.

## Fiction and safety boundary

- Every custom combat actor and the **Grey Wolf** hero is fictional. Grey Wolf
  is a role archetype with a face-obscuring helmet and generic bilingual voice;
  it is not based on any political leader, service member, or public figure.
- The mission **Straits Shield** takes place on a synthetic coastline against the
  fictional, stateless **Orion Group**. Geography, objectives, unit positions,
  force composition, timing, and outcome are invented.
- The mission is framed as protection of an evacuation corridor, harbor control,
  and fictional undersea infrastructure. It does not reproduce a recent attack,
  name a real adversary, or encode a current dispute.
- Source platform names are used only in this research record. Gameplay actors
  use original names, original visuals, and deliberately game-like capabilities.
- Public sources can become outdated and often mix demonstrated, planned, and
  marketed capability. This implementation treats them only as evidence for
  broad roles and preserves uncertainty instead of asserting readiness or exact
  inventory.
