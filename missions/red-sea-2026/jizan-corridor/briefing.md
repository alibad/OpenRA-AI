# Red Sea 2026: Jizan Corridor

Factual cutoff: 11 August 2026.

A regional escalation has interrupted radar coverage along the Jizan coastal
corridor while mobile launch systems threaten civilian navigation and port
logistics. Capture Radar Node Seven, establish layered air defense, destroy
both launchers, and escort the relief convoy into the port perimeter.

Your forward construction yard and refinery are operational. Expand the base,
establish a war factory, and replace losses as the corridor battle develops.

The background is source-dated. Force composition, routes, positions, timing,
and outcomes are fictional gameplay abstractions.

Sources:
