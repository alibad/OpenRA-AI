# Red Sea 2026: Hodeidah Lifeline

Factual cutoff: 11 August 2026.

Keep a coastal aid and fisheries corridor operational while fixed civilian
infrastructure and dispersed mobile forces face sustained pressure. Escort
relief supplies inland, move the mobile force out of the announced surveillance
zone, and protect the return route for the final evacuation convoy.

The playable Yemen base begins with a complete economy, ground-production
branch, radar, and airfield so the technical, mobile launcher, and Samad drone
all matter during the mission.

The background is source-dated. Force composition, routes, positions, timing,
and outcomes are fictional gameplay abstractions.

Sources:

- United Nations Development Programme, 28 June 2026;
- United Nations Office of the Special Envoy for Yemen, 24 July 2026.
