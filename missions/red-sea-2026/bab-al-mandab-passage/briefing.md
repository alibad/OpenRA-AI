# Red Sea 2026: Bab al-Mandab Passage

Factual cutoff: **11 August 2026**. Sources were last checked on that date.

## Sourced background

Bab al-Mandab is a strait between the Arabian Peninsula and the Horn of Africa
that connects the Red Sea with the Gulf of Aden and Arabian Sea. The UK
Permanent Committee on Geographical Names places Bab el Mandeb at approximately
12°35′N, 43°21′E and identifies Jazīrat Mayyūn (Perim Island) inside the strait.
The U.S. Energy Information Administration describes it as a strategic energy
route and estimated that 4.2 million barrels per day of crude oil and petroleum
products transited it in the first half of 2025. UNCTAD reported that the strait
carried 8.7% of global seaborne trade volume in 2023, including substantial
container, vehicle, oil-product, and crude-oil shares. On 23 July 2026, the
International Maritime Organization again called the Red Sea a critical
international trade corridor and emphasized that seafarers are civilians.

## Fictional mission situation

A Saudi maritime-security post must establish a coastal picture before a
civilian merchant group enters the strait. Build through the normal economy to
a Radar Dome and Tech Center, produce an additional M1A2S or SADS, reconnoiter
three coastal sectors, neutralize mobile launchers, protect four merchant
vessels in separate lanes around the stylized Mayyun island, and keep Passage
Control open during the final escalation.

All forces, weapons, threats, routes, incidents, positions, timings, dialogue,
and outcomes are fictional gameplay abstractions. The mission does not depict
or claim a real operation.
