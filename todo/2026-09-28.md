# 2026-09-28

## United States concept-board generator (Checkpoint B)

### What We Built

- `scripts/build-usa-concept-board.py` renders the United States (`usa`)
  Checkpoint B review boards into the engine's `docs/concept/usa/`: the
  three-theatre roster line-up, facings and states, the mandatory
  `USMBT`/`M1A2S` and `USICV`/`ARAS8` comparisons, player-colour zones, and a
  silhouette overlap matrix, each at native scale and 3x, plus a manifest with
  input and output SHA-256 digests.
- `scripts/usa_concept_actors.py` authors original low-poly geometry for all 21
  actors in `faction-spec-usa.md` §3, including the gameplay states that change
  the outline (launcher raised, pod raised, mast stowed, boom working, doors
  open, ramp lowered, launch and deploy poses). Landmark comments trace each
  shape to its contract line.
- `scripts/usa_concept_models.py` renders that geometry from a fixed camera for
  every facing, records authored player-colour faces in a mask, and quantizes
  frames into indexed Red Alert sprites (transparency 0, shadow 4, player colour
  on the 80-95 ramp by brightness, rotating and reserved indexes excluded).
  `scripts/usa_concept_render.py` ports OpenRA's `PlayerColorRemap`.
- `tests/test_usa_concept_models.py` pins the renderer contract.

### What We Fixed

- None in shipped assets. The U.S. renderer reuses the Red Sea `Mesh` but culls
  and depth-sorts against the camera its projection implies; the shared
  `_render` uses a mirrored camera and paints north faces over roofs. That and
  two other art-audit observations (≈55% accidental remap on `M1A2S`/`ARAS8`,
  culled `SAHINX`/`F15SA` wings) are recorded in the engine README for the art
  audit rather than changed here.

### Key Decisions

- Concept frames are real indexed sprites so the board can evolve into the
  shipping generator: the same geometry, quantizer and remap zones will feed
  the full 32-facing sheets once the product owner approves the board.
- Westwood-derived references are only drawn as scale ghosts or silhouettes,
  and terrain is synthesized from clear-tile index statistics, so no stock art
  is copied into the committed boards. The local image model was not used.
- OpenRA.Utility runs against a private scratch support directory that mirrors
  the read-only `Content/ra/v2`; the real support directory is never written.

### Verification

- `python -m pytest tests/test_usa_concept_models.py`: 47 passed.
- Generated the engine boards at product commit `6ce701d`; a second run into a
  fresh work directory produced byte-identical output for all ten PNGs.
- Boards were inspected at native scale and 3x; scale was checked against

  boxes (see the engine README's scale table).
