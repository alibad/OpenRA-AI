# 2026-09-05

## Complete, coherent RA2 armies and experience composition

- China now has 17 original combat/support units and three defenses; Iran has
  14 and three. Turkey retains its full 16-unit/three-defense roster. Together
  they provide 47 units and nine defenses, with distinct infantry, armor,
  artillery, aircraft, naval and support roles instead of four signature tanks.
  Shared native economy/utility buildings remain; original nine countries
  retain their combat statistics and country-specific units.
- China's portable AT/AA teams, deployed network technicians, amphibious
  carriers, airlift, ASW ships and carrier wings support networked formations.
  Iran combines stationary ambush bonuses, fragile drone coordinators, salvos,
  expendable loiter strikes, submarine ambushes and coastal denial.
  Turkey emphasizes mechanized infantry, designation, mobile screening and
  naval escorts. Minimum ranges, target restrictions, power, radar and Battle
  Lab requirements give opponents concrete counters and preserve progression.
- All recruitment tabs, starting armies, full-roster previews, native animated
  infantry/defenses, voxel models, 60x48 cameos and original bilingual voices
  are connected. Corrected inward-facing submarine hull and aircraft wing
  normals found in actual GPU captures, thickened undersampled rotor blades,
  and added readable defense panel/vent detail. No proprietary game assets
  were added to source or packaging.
- Final native visual review also repaired the inherited Turkey ship-deck
  perimeter and Sahin wing/fuselage normals: the dark triangles and black wing
  surfaces are gone without changing materials or the shared classic art.
- The default Modern Factions experience includes optional Combined-arms AI.
  Original Armies, Smarter Bots adds the same role-aware production to the
  original countries; Original Countries preserves upstream behavior. Existing
  custom selections are preserved. Bot profiles have different role weights
  and configurable 3–20-unit formation sizes; this is not omniscient targeting.
- Fixed faction overlays overwriting one another's air/naval/defense lists and
  stock-unit exclusion gates. Country prerequisites compose in a shared
  foundation without enabling disabled factions.
- Engine c3eb3dd94f fixes dependency/conflict resolution, inactive parameter
  effects and misleading capability counts. Authoring contracts are labelled
  separately from playable modules; actual powers describe their controls and
  limits. Role-only recruits now count toward production shares/limits,
  defense buildings do not inflate army counts, and unavailable aircraft do
  not stall other eligible recruits.
- Engine 97631433cb fixes a live production-starvation bug: factory-built
  aircraft no longer lose their recruitment share indefinitely as infantry
  and naval forces grow. Role weights are normalized within each production
  queue; legacy actor-specific production is unchanged. Turkey's Turna gunship
  is classified as strike aircraft, not as a cargo transport.
- Original-country role assignments follow actual weapons: Tanya is not an
  anti-tank recruit, Tesla tanks are direct-fire anti-armor, Sea Scorpion/Aegis
  provide AA, Black Eagle is a ground-strike aircraft, and the carrier supplies
  long-range naval artillery. Removed metadata-only V3/Dreadnought placeholders
  because this upstream adapter does not implement those buildable actors.
- Original snipers, desolators, bomb specialists and mind-control infantry keep
  positive recruitment roles under the optional doctrine instead of being
  silently skipped. Small bot-only specialist caps are conservative preview
  tuning; human unit rules remain unchanged, including existing dog and elite
  unit limits.
- AI unit/building observations now aggregate active secondary weapons and
  recognize surface/underwater/structure targets. Switching attack modes
  updates the companion's view without revealing hidden enemy information.
- Far-range tests exposed ground missiles inheriting unsuitable aircraft
  launch/flight settings. Corrected launch angles, cruise altitude and travel
  reserve so the advertised weapon ranges are usable, with dedicated
  stationary firing tests. Long descriptions now use native multiline
  tooltips instead of extending beyond the screen.
